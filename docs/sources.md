## Room 01 — Ono (1987)

Ono, K. (1987). Superstitious behavior in humans. *Journal of the
Experimental Analysis of Behavior, 47*(3), 261–271.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1348309

| Shown to the player | Status |
|---|---|
| Twenty students | verified (abstract) |
| Booth with three levers, a counter, a red lamp | verified (abstract: three levers, points on a counter with a red lamp and a buzzer) |
| Points given by a timer, nobody asked to do anything | verified (abstract: response-independent fixed-time or variable-time schedules of 30 s and 60 s; subjects not asked to perform any behavior) |
| A stable ritual in three of the twenty | verified (abstract: three subjects developed persistent superstitious behavior) |
| One of the three touched many things in the booth | verified (abstract: one subject "touched many things in the experimental booth"; the others: long pulls after a few short pulls, and "sensory superstition"). The earlier claim that a student jumped to touch the ceiling was not found in the abstract and was removed |
