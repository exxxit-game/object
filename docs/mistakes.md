# Mistakes and their guards

A mistake is written here only together with the guard that now catches it. A row
without a working guard is not allowed (`tests/structure.test.mjs` checks that each
row names an existing file, and caps this file at 60 lines). No diary: replace rows
when a better guard appears.

| Mistake (what happened) | Guard that catches it now |
|---|---|
| Designed a room or recommended an experiment before reading its paper in full: a 40-minute experiment was planned, then compressed into a different one; a research summary overstated an effect and it was recommended as the first room (pseudo-haptic weight: 4 of 8 noticed unprompted) | `.claude/skills/new-room/SKILL.md` step 1: no design and no recommendation before the full paper is read |
| The room told the player what the paper did not say: a number belonged to another group or sample (380 vs 242 people; "men" meaning 8 non-depressed men), and a sentence of the original instructions was dropped and not declared | `src/rooms/01-control/original.js` + `tests/control-protocol.test.mjs`; `.claude/agents/paper-reviewer.md` |
| A closed question lacked the valid answer, and options had unequal size and fixed order (bias) | `src/engine/ui/choice.js` (common size) + `tests/smoke.mjs` (size check); `.claude/agents/paper-reviewer.md` |
| Answer screens were crammed: button labels shrank to unreadable (margins ate the height), buttons covered the question text, and five consent paragraphs with two buttons were put on one wall board (two narrow columns, 19 px letters, CI red); typed in the wider typewriter face, the cover's blank after «Участник» wrapped onto a line of its own | `src/engine/panel.js` (`write` returns the text end, flags a blank wrapped away from its words); `tests/smoke.mjs`: no button over the text, no blank on a line of its own, a minimum answer text size, every sheet page fits, letters at least 1.2° and buttons 2.5° of view, up to four answers in one column; consent is paged (`src/app/consent.js`) |

| Code comments broke CLAUDE.md: Russian words slipped in, and comments told who asked or chose ("chosen by the owner", "the owner pointed the stubs out") | `tests/structure.test.mjs` rules 3 and 16 (no Russian in code comments; no dates and no note of who asked or chose in comments of src, tools and tests) |
