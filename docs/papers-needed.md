# Papers we could not get legally

These experiments cannot be recommended until the full text is read. Each needs a
legal copy saved as `C:\Users\admin\Documents\objekt-papers\<file>.pdf` (or put in
Downloads with that name); then the card is written from it (`docs/cards/README.md`).

Citations and DOIs were checked in Crossref (Ringelmann 1913 is not in Crossref; the link
searches Gallica). Legal ways, cheapest first: "Scholar" may show a free [PDF] on the right;
write to the corresponding author (address on the DOI page) or "Request full-text" on
ResearchGate; a library; buying the article. Never Sci-Hub or LibGen, never around a
CAPTCHA or bot check (the owner opens such pages in his own browser).

Received from the owner (cards written): chandler-2015, duncker-1945, easton-shor-1975,
fehr-gachter-2002, held-2011, huang-2022, jones-2008, kinateder-2014, latane-1979,
sebanz-2003, wang-2023, weijers-2014, wiltermuth-heath-2009, and a library of 21
evacuation papers (`objekt-papers\evacuation-library`).

Found free copies (cards not written yet; titles and authors checked on the first pages):
pronin-2002 (Rutgers course site); glucksberg-1962 and fitts-1954 (archive.org scans of
J Exp Psychol, US public domain: copyrights not renewed; scans, text needs OCR);
vonasch-2023 (authors' OSF, CC BY); lenggenhager-2007 (EPFL repository); bahrami-2010
(Aarhus repository); shelton-mcnamara-2001 (McNamara's lab page); holway-boring-1941
(University of Trieste course site, US public domain); simons-wang-1998, rieser-1989,
aglioti-1995 (a researcher's library linked by Google Scholar); navarrete-2012 (APA page
proof from the author's own site, kept only by the Wayback Machine: owner decides).
