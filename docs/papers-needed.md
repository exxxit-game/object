legal copy saved as `C:\Users\admin\Documents\objekt-papers\<file>.pdf`; then the
card is written or rewritten from it (`docs/cards/README.md`).

Titles and DOIs were checked in Crossref. Legal ways, cheapest first: write to the corresponding author (address on the
journal page) or press "Request full-text" on ResearchGate; a public or university
library (interlibrary loan); buying the single article from the publisher. Never
Sci-Hub or LibGen.
